# Data Infrastructure
The team gives a method called Efficient Data Infrastructure, it has composed of four core.
In 2025, you can see a lot of technical reports were emphasize the data quality rather than data quantity. The main reason is that the bottleneck in large (languange) model development has shifted from simply acquiring more training data to extracting more learning value from the data available.

## Data profiling engine
Extracts and compute a multi-dim features for each image-text pair, enable principled data curation. For example:

1. **Image Metadata**
resolution, file size, byte stream... Enabling nearly indentical images deduplicate.
2. **Technical Quality Assessment**
To filter low quality assets, with
- calculate the ratio of ideal uncompressed file size and actual file size, the lower the value, the lower the quality.
- quality assessment model to score images. Including color cast, blurriness, perceptible watermarks and excessive noise.w
- filter out low entropy images with analyzing the bording pixels, uniform-color backgrounds, and re-encode jpeg, use bytes-per-pixel as the complexity of image.
3. **Semantic and Aesthetic Content**
- aesthetics scoring model to quantify the visual applea of images
- dedicated classifier model to filter AI-gernerated content
- specialized Vision-Language-Model to generate semantic tags, include object category, human-centric attributes, element relevant objects, nsfw
4. **Cross-Modal Consistency and Captioning**
- use CN-CLIP to compute score between image and its captions, remove low score pairs
- structured (maybe json) captions, use VLM instead of OCR to process watermarks and text, integrate it to title

## Cross-modal vector engine
Build billions of embeddings for deduplication, with a high efficoent k-NN search function.
Moreover, they constructed an efficient restrieval pipeline leveraging multimodal features combined with a state-of-the-art index algorithm, for querying the failure of the system. Precise the position of datasets.

## World knowledge topological graph
Identify and fill conceptual voids in dataset, balancing the data distribution.
They build a knowledge graph from Wikipedia entities and their hyperlink structures. Then prune with two-pronged strategy: removes nodes with low PageRank scores, which means isolated concepts; use a VLM to discard abstract or ambiguous concepts. Subsequently, they use internal captioned images and texts to expand the knowledge graph. Then named the parent node to summarize its child nodes. Finally, select the graph with concepts randomly to ensure trending concepts not present in their data pool to maintain the relevance and timeless of the graph.

## Active curation engine
They establish a human-in-the-loop active learning cycle. Firstly, employ the topology graph and the initial reward model to curate a blanced subset from unlabeled media pool. Subsequently, they are labeled with a tag, and assigned a mechanism, which comprising both human and AI. The rejected samples as a correct to human verifier. This data is then used to reprogress the reward model and captioner. Form a cycle.
The system above facilitate the training of captioners, reward models and Z-Image-Edit

## Efficient Construction of Editing Pairs with Graphical Representatin

### Mixed Editing with Expert Models
Curating a diverse taxonomy of editing tasks, and then leverage task-specific expert models to synthesize data for each catogory. Simultaneously, multiple editing actions are integrated into one editing pair.

### Efficient Graphical Representation
Synthesizing multiple edit versions, corresponding to different editing tasks, enhancing the training efficiency. Also, they inverse pairs, transform a real input image to as an output image.

### Paired Image from Videos
To overcome the diversity of editing pairs from predefined tasks are limited. The videos equipped with complex sense, relation, style inherently. There are some advantage of video: high task diversity; inherent coupling of multiple edit types; superior scalability.

### Rendering for Text Editing
They draw on technique of Qwen-Image. For more information, please refer to [Qwen-Image](https://voluxium.github.io/hub/post/260914/) or the original technique report [Qwen-Image](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02324).

# Model Training

## Architecture
Qwen3-4B for text encoder, Flux.1 VAE for vae, SigLIP 2 for visual semantics.
The main dit architecture is Scalable Single-Stream Multi-Modal Diffusion Transformer (S3-DiT). In this setup, text, latent image, visual semantic tokens are concatenated at the sequence level to serve as a unified input stream.
![architecture of Z-image](https://img.scatter.cc.cd/z-image/architecture.png)
You can see that all the input elements except time are concatenated in one vector. Different from dual-stream mmdit, it gives image and text seperately, then they interact in Joint Attention.

In training strategy, they freeze the parameters of text encoder and vae, implement gradient checkpointing across all dit layers, compile the model with torch.compile(). Also, they achieve a sequence length-aware batch construction strategy. Smaller batch size are assigned to long-sequence batch and on the contrary, avoid resource vacancy.

## Pre-training
Z-image also uses flow-matching objective, and dynamic time shifting strategy as Flux.

### Low-resolution Pre-training
This phase accounts for over half of total pre-training compute. For boarding the width of knowledge that model get.

### Omni-pre-training
1. The model is trained on images with diverse resolutions and ratios.
2. Joint t2i and i2i task. They found that it does not degrade the performance of model obviously.
3. Generating multi-level synthetic captions with Z-Captioner. In addition, the metadata of image is associated with images with a small probability. 

## Supervised Fine-Tuning
1. Although the omni pre-training establishes board world understanding, the data distribution has nosie because of the web scale data may not absolutely balanced. In SFT, model is dominated by highly curated images (Internal high quaility data). Forcing the model to discard low-quality data.
2. Narrowing the distribution is a risk of catastrophic forgetting. They employ a dynamic resampling strategy guided by world knowledge topological graph, and based on BM25, retrieval to compute rarity scores for training samples. Then construct mini-batches, increasing the weight of underrepresented concepts and decreasing the weight of overrepresented concepts.
3. Training the model into multiple SFT variants. Then merge the model weight in parameter space.

## Few-Step Distillation
For reducing the inference time, although the parameters is just 6B.
The distillation involves teaching a student model to learn the teacher's denoising across fewer step. They initially selected Distribution Matching Distillation (DMD) paradigm but it caused loss of high-frequency detials, noticeable color shifts.
They improved DMD: Decoupled DMD and DMDR. These core distill techniques used in building Z-Image-Turbo

### Decoupled DMD: Resolving Detial and Color Degradation
Current effectiveness of DMD is the result of two indenpent mechanisms:
CFG-Augmentation: It acts as the primary engine to build the few-step of distillation, which has been ignored in previous literature.
Distribution Matching: A regularizer, ensuring the stability of training process and removing the emerging artifacts.
It's a improved distillation framework, ensuring sharp detail presercation and color fidelity. The result model not only matches the origin mutil-step teacher but even surpasses it in term of photorealism and visual impact
![Decoupled DMD](https://img.scatter.cc.cd/z-image/decoupled-dmd.webp)

### DMDR: Enhancing Capacity with RL and Regularization
To push the performance boundaries of turbo model, they integrate RL into distillation process. However, apply RL into generative model faces 'reward hacking', where the model generates nonsensical image but get high-scroing. So they combine the DMD function and RL. In this mechanism, RL unlock the student capacity align with human reference, while DM term acts as a constraint, preventing reward hacking. It turns the Z-Image-Turbo possesses both aesthetic and gernerate stability.
![DMDR](https://img.scatter.cc.cd/z-image/DMDR.webp)

## RLHF
DPO and GRPO, both are standard operating procedures in morden model

### Offline Alignment with DPO on Objective Dimension
Manually curating preference for DPO is slow. They DPO pivots to focus on objective, verifiable dimention, such as text rendering and object counting, with their powerfull VLM to evaluate the model gerneration. Also verified with human.
Futhermore, they implement a curriculum learning strategy for DPO. The process begin with low complexity and progressively advanced to high complexity like more elements, styles, layouts. They found that DPO is sensitive to the difference of positive and negative samples.

### Online Refinement with GRPO
Guilded by reward model, this stage is designed to enhance the capability of photorealistic and aesthetic. They compute advantage function in multiple dimentions, to provide signal in different aspects. It proves to be more effective than single reward.
