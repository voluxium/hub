# Lead-in
Before it, I will give you a little story about myself.
AI have grown fast in 2023, and it also attact more and more people into it. In 2022, stability ai released a open-source model called stable diffusion, then they released some high popularity model like sd1.5, sdxl. All of these model could generate delicate image, which attacted artists into it.
I have got interesting into AI since mid-2025(that was the era of sdxl and flux.1), and I search many theory about it, I want to complete a basic full-stack learning source on basic artificial intellgence. Also, I came into contact with AI gernerate image, when I first download models, configurate the comfyui, and click 'run'. A crude photograph amazed me, about a bottle filled with the starry sky(In comfyui, the default prompt is this.), then a long-term research lead me into comfyui, from unet, clip, vae, to ksampler, condition, scheduler, more and more complex workflow flowed to me. Several month have passed, I could connect a advanced workflow to control any image I want, also understand how artificial intellegence generates a image, how tensors compute in the model, essence is a martrix. It seemed the interest guide me to learn AI through comfyui, but do you remember my origin goals?

# Lost origin goal
In third paragraph of Lead-in, I claimed the goal: Complete a basic full-stack learning source on basic artificial intellgence. However, do you think that the initial goal has been lost?
You may raise questions. The comfyui was a AI project that helps you learned the structure of model, you even created a complex workflow in it, understood how model compute a image, doesn't these included in you goal.
It is the biggest point of contention in this matter. Although I got some knowledge from comfyui, but my initial goal was learn a basic full stack of AI, comfyui only participated in a part. My attention turn into a specific field and delay the full-stack learning for several month. `The most crucial changes is that the learner's interests doesn't change, but the served object by interests is changed.` This is very discreet, the person may think, upgrade, understand more and more continuously, but hard to realize the lose of full progress.

# Why did this happen?
Local interest replaces overall goals.
1. A person establish a goal firstly, but at that time, it was macro. Then he learned the base of knowledge system, the relationship of these.
2. Secondly, the local interest actived. It brought continuous curiosity to himself. Compared with other field, seemed more boring, futher, even unnessary for now.
3. Patience, attention come to shift to local direction, breadth to depth.

# What will it lead to?
Their progress id real. They understand concepts they once found incomprehensible, solve increasingly increasingly diffcult problems, and develop a deeper appreciation of the subject. Yet something else is happening: other areas of subject are being neglected. The broader foundantion they originally intended to build remains incomplete. Month or years later, they may hava become quite knowledgeable in one specialized area without having developed the overall understanding they initially sought.
More seriously, this mechanism can reinforce himself. The more time they spend in the field they selected, the more familiar concepts they get. Studying will become naturally and rewarding, while switching to an unfamiliar area requires more effort and bring less satisfaction compared to initial. Progressed Knowledge will give them the reason they walk along the path deviant and deviant.

# How to achieve a breakthough?
The solution is not to compress the curiosity to avoid. Deep exploration is offen essential to mastery, an unexpect interest may achieve a more worthy goal compare with initial. **The key is to distinguish between a deliberate change of objective and ana unexamined drift away from it**. It required us regularly focus on the present and asking questions frequently. We must not only ask our current work is intersting whether or not, but also requires for whether it achieves the desired results. Sometimes the most difficult deviation to recognize is the one that still looks like progress.

In my worldview, the only indicator of something is successful is whether its purpose has been achieved.
